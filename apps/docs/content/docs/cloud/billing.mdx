FishMem Cloud billing runs on monthly Stripe subscriptions. You pick a plan,
the plan sets your monthly credit capacity, and you manage everything through the
Stripe customer portal from the dashboard.

## Plans

| Plan | Price (monthly) |
|---|---|
| Hobby | Free |
| Starter | $19 |
| Growth | $79 |
| Pro | $249 |
| Enterprise | Custom |

Start on **Hobby** for free to evaluate. **Starter**, **Growth**, and **Pro**
step up the monthly credit capacity as your usage grows. **Enterprise** is
custom — talk to us if you need volume, terms, or arrangements beyond Pro.

## How plans relate to credits

Your plan is the bucket; credits are the meter. Each plan includes a monthly
credit allotment, and metered operations draw down against it. Moving up a tier
raises your monthly capacity. For what each operation costs and how to estimate
spend, see [Usage and credits](/cloud/usage-and-credits).

## Managing your subscription

Subscriptions are handled through Stripe. From the dashboard, open the **Stripe
customer portal** to:

- Upgrade or downgrade your plan.
- Update your payment method.
- View invoices and billing history.
- Cancel your subscription.

Because billing goes through Stripe's portal, your card details and invoices are
managed by Stripe directly — FishMem doesn't handle raw payment data.

## Changing plans

When you change plans, your monthly credit capacity changes with it. Upgrade when
you're consistently bumping against your allotment; the [usage views](/cloud/usage-and-credits)
in the dashboard show how much headroom you have so you can time the move.
